# Day 10 Cheat Sheet — Learning How to Learn

## The Five Techniques
| Technique | Core idea | In your curriculum |
|---|---|---|
| Spaced repetition | review at 1d/3d/7d/14d — flatten forgetting curve | Review days, mistake-log re-tests |
| Active recall | retrieval builds memory; re-reading doesn't | Mastery checks "from memory" |
| Deliberate practice | train at the edge of ability | Hard Mode, combined-concept exercises |
| Interleaving | mix topics; force tool-selection | Review days, bridge.py, projects |
| Feynman technique | explain simply or you don't know it | Weekly communication artifacts |

## Metacognition states
- KNOWN = demonstrated under verification
- CLAIMED = feels familiar ← danger zone
- UNKNOWN = schedule it, no shame

## The fluency illusion
"I've seen this" ≠ "I can produce this."
Defeat it: test yourself BEFORE checking. Always.

## Personal protocol (7 steps)
learn small → retrieve immediately → practice at edge → interleave → explain → space → log failures

## AI-era rule
attempt → hint (not answer) → verify everything → periodic AI-free proof
