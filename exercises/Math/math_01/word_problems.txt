Word problems: 
(a) I spent 150 pesos and have 350 left — how much did I start with? 
(b) Three times my age is 60. How old am I? 
(c) Half of a number is 12. What is the number? Translate, solve, check.

(a) x - 150 = 350 
x + 150 - 150 = 350 + 150
x = 500

(b) 3x = 60
3x / 3 = 60 / 3
x = 20

(c) (1/2)x = 12
(1/2)x / (1/2) = 12 / (1/2)
x = 12 * 2/1
x = 24
